# 5E: Resolution

## D20 Tests
Every uncertain action uses a d20 roll plus a modifier, compared with a target number. You explain; the GM sets the target and reads the roll.

| Test | When | Roll | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ability check | a character tries something with a meaningful chance of failure | d20 + ability modifier (+ Proficiency Bonus if proficient in the skill or tool) | DC set by the GM |
| Saving throw | a character resists a threat (a trap, poison, a spell) | d20 + ability modifier (+ Proficiency Bonus if proficient in that save) | DC set by the effect or the GM |
| Attack roll | a character tries to hit | d20 + ability modifier + Proficiency Bonus (if proficient) | the target's Armor Class |

**Call for a roll only when** the outcome is uncertain and failure would matter. Otherwise the character just succeeds (or just can't), and say so.

## Abilities and skills
| Ability | Covers | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | physical power | Athletics |
| Dexterity | agility, reflexes, balance | Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth |
| Constitution | health, stamina | — (saves and endurance checks) |
| Intelligence | reasoning, memory | Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion |
| Wisdom | perception, intuition | Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival |
| Charisma | force of personality | Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion |

The skill follows from *how* the character does it, as the player describes it. Shoving a door is Strength (Athletics); finding the hidden latch is Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception). The GM may pair a skill with a different ability when the fiction calls for it (e.g. Strength (Intimidation)).

Saving throws by ability: Strength resists force, Dexterity dodges harm, Constitution endures toxins and strain, Intelligence sees through illusions, Wisdom resists mental assault, Charisma holds on to one's self.

## Advantage and Disadvantage
- Advantage: roll two d20s, use the higher. Disadvantage: roll two, use the lower.
- They don't stack: many sources still mean two dice. If a roll has both, it has neither.
- The GM can grant Advantage or impose Disadvantage for circumstances (a clever plan, bad footing, darkness). This is the main way a scene feature changes a roll.
- **Help:** a character with a relevant skill or tool proficiency who helps an ally gives Advantage on the ally's next check with it. Helping can also distract a foe and give an ally Advantage on an attack.

## Other rules worth reminding
- **Natural 20 / natural 1:** on an attack roll, a 20 always hits (a Critical Hit: damage dice are rolled twice) and a 1 always misses. On ability checks and saving throws they have no special effect.
- **Passive score:** 10 + the check's total modifier (Passive Perception is the usual one); +5 with Advantage, −5 with Disadvantage. Use it to decide whether someone notices something without rolling.
- **Opposed checks (GM technique):** when two creatures oppose each other directly, both can roll the relevant check and the higher total wins; on a tie, nothing changes. Many 5E rules instead set the DC from the opponent's score (e.g. a hiding creature's check total becomes the DC to find it).
- **Group checks (GM technique):** when everyone attempts the same thing, the group can succeed if at least half succeed.

## Social checks
- An NPC's attitude is **Friendly** (Advantage on checks to influence it), **Indifferent** or **Hostile** (Disadvantage).
- Asking something the NPC is **willing** to do needs no roll; something **unwilling** (against its nature or interest) fails without a roll; only a **hesitant** NPC calls for a check.
- Default DC for a hesitant NPC: 15, or the NPC's Intelligence score if higher. Checks by approach: Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion, or Animal Handling for beasts.
- After a failed attempt, the same request can't be made again for about a day, unless the situation changes.

## Heroic Inspiration
A character holds at most one. Spending it rerolls any one die right after it is rolled; the new roll stands. The GM awards it for heroic, in-character or entertaining play.

## Answer shape for /help-skills
1. **Test:** ability check, saving throw or attack roll.
2. **Fitting checks:** 2-3 ability (skill) pairs, best fit first, each with the fiction that makes it fit ("climbing fast is Strength (Athletics); finding the safe handholds first is Wisdom (Perception)"). The player's description decides; say so.
3. **Suggested DC:** a value from the table with the reason, or the opponent's check for a contest.
4. **Advantage / Disadvantage:** what in the scene would grant or impose it, if anything.
5. **Rule hooks:** a matching class feature, feat or condition from the PC's file; never quote a spell or item, only name it.
