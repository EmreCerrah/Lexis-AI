# Doom d20: Resolution

## Tests
Roll d20 + the fitting ability. Meeting or beating the **Difficulty Rating (DR)** succeeds. A natural 20 is a **crit** (an extra benefit), a natural 1 a **fumble** (an extra disaster). You explain; the GM sets the DR and reads the roll.

## Abilities
| Ability | Covers |
|---|---|
| Strength | lifting, breaking, climbing, melee attacks |
| Agility | dodging, sneaking, balance, defending in a fight |
| Presence | perception, wits, charm, ranged attacks, powers |
| Toughness | enduring poison, cold, hunger, falls, pain |

Each ability is a modifier from −3 to +3. The ability follows from *how* the character does it, as the player describes it.

## Difficulty Rating
| DR | Task |
|---|---|
| 6 | very simple |
| 8 | simple |
| 10 | routine |
| 12 | normal |
| 14 | difficult |
| 16 | really hard |
| 18 | should hardly be possible |

Call for a test only when failure would matter. Most tests are DR 12; move the DR rather than adding modifiers.

## Combat basics
- **Initiative:** the GM rolls d6: 1-3 the enemies act first, 4-6 the PCs act first (individual initiative is an option).
- **Attack:** melee is Strength against DR 12, ranged is Presence against DR 12. A hit rolls the weapon's damage (d4 to d10), reduced by the target's armor.
- **Defend:** a PC attacked by a foe tests Agility against DR 12 (higher for stronger foes); failure means the PC takes the hit. The GM never rolls to hit the PCs.
- **Crit / fumble in a fight:** a crit attack does double damage (and can damage armor); a crit defense allows a free counter-attack; a fumble attack can break or lose the weapon; a fumble defense can mean double damage.
- **Armor tiers:** light, medium, heavy, reducing damage by a die (d2, d4, d6); heavier armor makes Agility tests (defense included) harder by +2 or +4 DR. A crit against the wearer can knock armor down a tier.

## Omens
Each PC starts each day with a few Omens (d2 by default; classes may change it). Spending one gives the player a lucky break, for example a reroll, less damage taken, or a lowered DR; the exact list is in the GM's book. Omens return after a night's rest.

## Answer shape for /help-skills
1. **Test:** which ability, 2 fitting options at most, each with the fiction that makes it fit ("Strength to force the grate; Agility to squeeze through the bars"). The player's description decides; say so.
2. **Suggested DR:** a value from the table, with the reason.
3. **Crit / fumble:** one short line each for what they would mean here.
4. **Options:** an Omen that would help, armor or gear that changes the DR, a class ability or power from the PC's file (by name).

🎲 <Strength test>
