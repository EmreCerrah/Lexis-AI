# d100: Resolution

## The roll
Roll d100 (two d10: tens and ones; 00 means 100). A result **equal to or under** the chance succeeds. You explain; the GM sets the chance and reads the roll.

| Roll | Chance |
|---|---|
| Skill roll | the skill rating (%) |
| Characteristic roll | characteristic × 5 (Effort STR, Stamina CON, Idea INT, Luck POW, Agility DEX, Charm CHA; Knowledge EDU if used) |
| Resistance roll | from the resistance table: active factor against passive factor |

**No roll** for routine actions (Automatic) or truly impossible ones. Call for a roll only when the outcome is uncertain and matters.

## Five success levels
| Level | When |
|---|---|
| Critical success | roll ≤ 1/20 of the chance (round up): the best possible result; a weapon ignores armor and does maximum damage |
| Special success | roll ≤ 1/5 of the chance: better than average; in combat an impale or knockdown |
| Success | roll ≤ the chance |
| Failure | roll > the chance; 96-00 always fails |
| Fumble | the top 1/20 of the failure range (usually 96-00 to 00); 00 always fumbles; a disaster beyond simple failure |

A skill with a base chance of 5% or more always succeeds on 01-05, whatever the modifiers.

## Difficulty
| Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| Automatic | no roll |
| Easy | skill doubled (characteristic × 10); no experience check |
| Average | skill as is (characteristic × 5) |
| Difficult | skill halved, round up |
| Impossible | no roll (or a flat 01% if the GM allows) |

## Situational modifiers
Applied **after** Easy/Difficult. Typical sizes: **±20%** (poor tools, darkness, bad weather, rushed; or good tools, plenty of time, favorable conditions) and **±50%** (no tools at all, pitch black, utterly alien; or ideal conditions, routine and familiar). Kinds: task complexity, equipment, environment, familiarity, range, time, understanding. Don't stack many: judge the overall modifier and roll.

## Resistance roll
Chance = 50% + (active − passive) × 5%. Equal factors: 50%. A difference of 10 or more is automatic success or failure. Examples: STR vs a stuck door's STR, Potency of a poison vs CON, POW vs POW, power points vs power points.

## Opposed rolls
Both roll their skills. The **higher success level** wins; a successful loser lowers the winner's level by one step for each level of success they achieved. Same level: the higher roll wins.

## Helping and combining
- **Cooperative roll:** helpers roll; the best result modifies the lead's skill: critical +50%, special +30%, success +20%, all failed −10%, any fumble −50%.
- **Complementary skill:** add 1/5 of a related skill's rating (only one).
- **Augment:** a successful roll of a related skill makes the main roll one step easier; a failed one, one step harder.
- **Combined roll:** doing two things at once uses the lower skill.

## Power points as fate points (optional)
If the GM uses it: spend 5 power points to reroll any percentile roll (the reroll stands); 3 to ignore 1 point of damage; 6 to shift a result one level better. At 0 power points the character faints.

## Answer shape for /help-skills
1. **Roll:** skill roll, characteristic roll or resistance roll.
2. **Fitting skills:** 2-3, best fit first, each with the fiction that makes it fit ("Climb for the wall; Jump to cross the gap; Agility roll if no skill fits"). The player's description decides; say so.
3. **Difficulty:** Easy / Average / Difficult, with the reason.
4. **Modifiers:** situational modifiers in the scene (±20%, ±50%), and a cooperative or augmenting skill if one fits.
5. **Levels:** one short line for what a special or critical success and a fumble would mean here.

```
🎲 <Skill roll: Climb>
- Skill: Climb — … · Jump — …
- Difficulty: Difficult (½) — …
- Modifiers: −20% rain-slick stone · augment: …
- Special/critical: … · Fumble: …
```
