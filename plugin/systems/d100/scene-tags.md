# d100: Scene Tags = Situational Modifiers

This system has no aspects. The scene tag is a **situational modifier**: a condition of the scene that adds to or subtracts from skill ratings. It always has a concrete size (±20% or ±50%) and names which rolls it affects. It should be usable by the PCs *and* able to hinder them.

## Kinds and sizes
| Kind | −50% | −20% | +20% | +50% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Task complexity | unfathomable, no solution in sight | needlessly complex | fairly straightforward | simple, obvious |
| Equipment | no equipment when it is needed | poor or improvised | high quality | advanced, ideal |
| Environment | pitch black, storm, unstable ground | darkness, bad footing, bad weather | good footing, space, quiet | ideal for the task |
| Familiarity | completely alien | strange, unfamiliar | well known | routine |
| Range | far beyond normal range | outside comfortable range | well within range | perfectly placed |
| Time | nowhere near enough | rushed | plenty of time | at leisure |
| Understanding | no way to communicate | limited communication | familiar and willing | enthusiastic |

A scene tag can also make a roll **Easy** or **Difficult** (a whole step) instead of a percentage.

## Other states worth tagging
- **Unconscious:** 2 or fewer hit points, or knocked out (Damage vs CON on the resistance table for knockout blows).
- **Prone, entangled, blinded (GM technique):** treat the hampered side's rolls as Difficult and rolls against it as Easy, unless the GM's book says otherwise.
- **Fatigue (optional):** fatigue points STR+CON; when exhausted, skills become Difficult or limited.
- **Temporary insanity (optional Sanity):** the character acts under a temporary insanity effect for its duration.

## A good scene tag
1. **Concrete and visible:** "Flooded Lower Stairs" beats "bad conditions". Name it in the world's voice and language.
2. **One clear effect:** a modifier from the table, or one step of difficulty, and which rolls it touches.
3. **Two-sided:** one line on how the PCs can use it, one on how it can work against them.
4. **True in the fiction:** it follows from what happened, obeys `## Immutable Rules`, and reveals no `## 🔒 Hidden Truth`.

## Output block
```
**"<Tag name>"** — <kind: complexity / equipment / environment / familiarity / range / time / understanding / state>
- Effect: <e.g. −20% to Spot and Listen; Climb is Difficult>
- Use it: <how a PC can exploit it, one line>
- Against them: <how it can hinder the PCs, one line>
```
