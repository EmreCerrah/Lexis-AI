# FitD: Obstacles

## Position and effect (suggest, never decide)
- **Position:** risky by default; controlled when the PCs act on their terms; desperate when they overreach or the danger is overwhelming.
- **Effect:** standard by default; great when the PCs have the advantage in potency, quality or scale; limited when they are outmatched.
- Rule of thumb: if nothing is at stake, don't call for a roll, and say so. A big obstacle is a **clock**, not one hard roll.

## Clocks
| Size | When |
|---|---|
| 4 segments | a complex obstacle |
| 6 segments | a more complicated one |
| 8 segments | a daunting one, or a long-term project |

- **Danger clock:** ticks 1, 2 or 3 on a minor, standard or serious complication; when full, the danger happens.
- **Racing clocks:** the PCs' clock against the opposition's; the first to fill wins.
- **Linked clocks:** a second clock opens once the first fills (break the defense, then reach the target).
- **Mission clock:** the time window; when it fills, the plan fails or changes.
- **Tug-of-war clock:** can fill and empty with events.
- Name a clock after the obstacle, not the method ("Vault Guards", not "Fight the Guards").

## Consequences (for 4-5 and 1-3)
| Consequence | What it does |
|---|---|
| Reduced effect | the action does less (one effect level lower) |
| Complication | new trouble, or a danger clock ticks (minor 1, standard 2, serious 3); never undoes a success |
| Lost opportunity | this approach no longer works; try another |
| Worse position | still possible, but now from a worse position |
| Harm | a lasting injury or debility: level 1 lesser, 2 moderate, 3 severe, 4 fatal |

A PC may **resist** any consequence: roll the attribute (Insight against deception and misunderstanding, Prowess against physical harm, Resolve against mental strain), take 6 minus the highest die in stress (a critical clears 1 stress), and the consequence is reduced or avoided (usually reduced). **Armor** can be marked instead of rolling.

## Obstacle types
| Type | What to produce |
|---|---|
| Barrier (lock, wall, chasm) | fitting actions with the fiction for each, suggested position and effect, what 4-5 and 1-3 would cost |
| Guarded place | a danger clock (e.g. "Alarm" 4 or 6), the factors (scale of the guards, quality of the locks), entry options |
| Puzzle / information | what Study, Survey or Attune can learn; gathering information: a 6 gives a clear answer, 4-5 a partial one |
| Hazard (fire, flood, collapse) | a danger clock, the harm level it inflicts, which attribute resists it |
| Social obstacle (crowd, gatekeeper) | what they want, Sway / Consort / Command and how each would play, the position, a clock if it takes time |
| Long challenge | a progress clock (6 or 8) ticked by effect, raced by a danger clock |

## Output block
```
**FitD**
- Tag: "<…>" — <factor or clock>
- Fitting actions: <Action> — <how>; <Action> — <how>
- Suggested position: <controlled / risky / desperate> — <why> · effect: <limited / standard / great> — <why>
- Clock: "<name>" (<4/6/8>) — <what happens when full>
- 4-5: <consequence> · 1-3: <consequence> (harm level <n>, resisted with <attribute>)
```
