# FitD: Resolution

## The roll
Roll a pool of d6 equal to the rating (usually 1-4 dice) and read the **single highest die**.

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Critical (two or more 6s) | full success with an extra advantage (increased effect) |
| 6 | full success |
| 4-5 | partial success: it happens, with a consequence |
| 1-3 | bad outcome |

**Zero dice:** roll 2d6 and take the **lowest**; no critical possible. You explain; the GM sets position and effect and reads the roll.

Kinds of roll: **action roll** (a PC does something risky), **resistance roll** (reduce a consequence), **fortune roll** (the GM leaves something to chance), **engagement roll** (how a plan starts) and downtime rolls.

## The 12 actions
| Attribute | Actions |
|---|---|
| Insight | **Hunt** (track, ambush, precise shooting), **Study** (research, read people), **Survey** (observe, spot danger and openings), **Tinker** (devices, locks, traps) |
| Prowess | **Finesse** (deft hands, misdirection, driving, dueling), **Prowl** (sneak, hide, climb, run), **Skirmish** (close fighting, wrestling, holding a position), **Wreck** (smash, sabotage, overwhelm) |
| Resolve | **Attune** (open the mind to the supernatural, if the world has it), **Command** (intimidate, order, lead), **Consort** (socialize, contacts), **Sway** (persuade, charm, lie) |

The action follows from *how* the character does it, as the player describes it. Players choose the action; the GM answers with position and effect.

## Position and effect
- **Position** (how dangerous): **Controlled** (on your terms), **Risky** (the default: head to head), **Desperate** (overreaching, serious trouble).
- **Effect** (how much it achieves): **Great**, **Standard** (the usual), **Limited**; it can drop to zero or rise to extreme. On a clock: limited ticks 1, standard 2, great 3 segments.
- Effect factors: **potency** (a weakness, extra time, arcane help), **quality** (tools, resources, tier), **scale** (numbers, area, reach).
- A player may **trade position for effect** (or effect for position) after the GM announces them.

## Outcomes by position
| | Controlled | Risky | Desperate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | increased effect | increased effect | increased effect |
| 6 | you do it | you do it | you do it |
| 4-5 | hesitate, or do it with a minor cost | do it, with harm, a complication, reduced effect or a worse position | do it, with severe harm, a serious complication and reduced effect |
| 1-3 | falter: take a risky opportunity or withdraw | things go badly: harm, complication, worse position, lost opportunity | the worst: severe harm, a serious complication, lost opportunity |

## Bonus dice and stress
- **Push yourself:** 2 stress for +1d, or +1 effect, or to act while incapacitated.
- **Devil's bargain:** +1d for a price offered by the GM or a player (collateral damage, a lost item, a clock ticks …), paid whatever the result. Push **or** bargain, not both.
- **Assist:** a teammate takes 1 stress and gives +1d (only one assist per roll); they may share the consequences.
- Normally at most two bonus dice.

## Teamwork
- **Group action:** everyone rolls the same action, the best result counts for all; the leader takes 1 stress for each PC who rolled 1-3.
- **Set up:** a successful indirect action gives the follow-up +1 effect or a better position.
- **Protect:** take a teammate's consequence (and resist it if you like).
- **Flashback:** act in the past to set something up; costs 0, 1 or 2 stress by how unlikely it is, and may need a roll.

## Fortune roll
When the outcome is left to chance (how loyal an NPC is, how far a fire spreads), roll dice equal to a fitting trait or magnitude (0 dice: 2 take lowest): critical extreme, 6 great/full, 4-5 standard/partial, 1-3 limited/bad.

## Answer shape for /help-skills
1. **Action:** 2-3 fitting actions, best fit first, each with the fiction that makes it fit ("Prowl to cross the rooftops; Finesse to bluff past on the cart"). The player chooses; say so.
2. **Suggested position:** controlled / risky / desperate, with the reason.
3. **Suggested effect:** limited / standard / great, with the factor (potency, quality, scale); a clock it would tick if there is one.
4. **Likely consequences on 4-5 and 1-3:** one short line each.
5. **Options:** push, assist, set up, a devil's bargain idea, a fitting special ability from the PC's file.
