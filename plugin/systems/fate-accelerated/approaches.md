# FAE: Approaches, Actions, Outcomes

## Approaches (Turkish gloss for answers)
| Approach | Türkçe | Fits when the character… |
|---|---|---|
| Careful | Dikkatli | takes time, pays attention to detail, does it properly and safely |
| Clever | Zeki | thinks fast, solves a problem, accounts for complex variables, uses knowledge |
| Flashy | Gösterişli | draws attention, acts with style and panache, impresses or distracts |
| Forceful | Güçlü | uses brute strength, pushes through, intimidates head-on |
| Quick | Hızlı | moves fast, reacts first, is nimble |
| Sneaky | Sinsi | misdirects, hides, deceives, acts unseen |

Standard character spread: one at Good (+3), two at Fair (+2), two at Average (+1), one at Mediocre (+0). Check `system.md` for Homebrew.

**The same action can use different approaches.** The approach is decided by *how* the character does it, described by the player. When answering "which skill/approach?", give 2-3 approaches with the fiction that would justify each, best fit first.

## Four actions
| Action | Türkçe | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Overcome | Engeli aş | get past an obstacle, achieve a goal |
| Create an Advantage | Avantaj yarat | create or discover an aspect, or get free invokes on an existing one |
| Attack | Saldır | try to harm someone (physically or mentally) |
| Defend | Savun | actively stop someone else's attack or advantage |

## Outcomes (the GM reads the roll; you only explain)
- **Fail:** fail, or succeed at a serious cost.
- **Tie:** succeed at a minor cost / get a boost instead.
- **Success (+1, +2):** succeed.
- **Success with style (+3 or more):** succeed and get an extra benefit (a boost, a free invoke).

## Stunts
Format: *"Because I [describe how I'm awesome], I get a +2 when I [approach] [action] when [circumstance]."* or *"Because I [...], once per session I can [do something cool]."* Look up the PC's stunts in their character file or `system.md` before answering — a matching stunt is the most useful thing to mention.

## Stress and consequences
- Stress: 3 boxes (1, 2, 3) by default.
- Consequences: mild (2), moderate (4), severe (6). A consequence is an aspect.
- Taken out: the attacker decides how the character leaves the scene (the GM rules).
- Concede: before the roll, the player gives up on their terms and gets fate points.

## Fate points
Refresh 3 by default. Invoke: spend 1 → +2 or reroll. Compel: GM offers 1 → aspect complicates life.
