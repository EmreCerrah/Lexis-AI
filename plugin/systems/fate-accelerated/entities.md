# FAE: Entity Sections

The core templates hold the fiction (`## Who`, `## Wants`, `## Links` …). This file says which **system sections** to add where a template has the `{{system sections}}` line.

## Character
| Section | NPC | PC | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| `## Aspects` | ✓ | ✓ | high concept, trouble, 0-3 others (see `scene-tags.md`) |
| `## Approaches` | — | ✓ | the six approaches with ratings |
| `## Stunts` | — | ✓ | 1-3 stunts |
| `## Stress` | — | ✓ | stress boxes and consequence slots |

A minor NPC needs only `## Aspects` (1-2). If an NPC will fight or oppose the PCs, add one line under it: `Good (+3) at: …; Poor (−1) at: …; stress: n`.

## Location
`## Aspects`: 1-2 permanent aspects of the place.

## Group
`## Aspects`: 1-2 permanent aspects of the group.

## Character math
- **Approaches:** one at Good (+3), two at Fair (+2), two at Average (+1), one at Mediocre (+0).
- **Refresh:** 3. Each stunt beyond the third costs 1 refresh (minimum refresh 1).
- **Stress:** 3 boxes (1, 2, 3).
- **Consequences:** mild (absorbs 2), moderate (4), severe (6). A consequence is an aspect.
- Check `system.md` Homebrew before using these numbers.

## Stunts
Format: *"Because I [describe how I'm awesome], I get a +2 when I [approach] [action] when [circumstance]."* or *"Because I [...], once per session I can [do something cool]."* Look up the PC's stunts before answering; a matching stunt is the most useful thing to mention.

## Harm
- Taken out: the attacker decides how the character leaves the scene (the GM rules on it).
- Concede: before the roll, the player gives up on their own terms and gets fate points.
- Recovery: mild consequences clear after a scene, moderate after a session, severe after a scenario, once treated in the fiction.
