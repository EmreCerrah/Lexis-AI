# FAE: Obstacles

## Choosing a difficulty (suggest, never decide)
| Situation | Suggested passive opposition |
|---|---|
| Easy but not automatic; failure would still be interesting | Average (+1) / Fair (+2) |
| A real challenge for a competent PC | Good (+3) / Great (+4) |
| Heroic, only with aspects invoked | Superb (+5) / Fantastic (+6) |
| Nearly impossible without a plan | Epic (+7) and up |

Rules of thumb: if failure is not interesting, don't call for a roll, and say so. Raise the difficulty by +2 for each strong, relevant situation aspect working against the PCs. Give a **range**, for example "Fair–Good (+2/+3)", and name what pushes it up or down.

## Passive vs active
- **Passive:** a fixed difficulty (a wall, a lock, a storm).
- **Active:** someone opposes with an approach roll (a guard, a cultist). Suggest the NPC's best approach and its rating.

## Obstacle types
| Type | What to produce |
|---|---|
| Barrier (lock, wall, chasm) | 1 aspect, a difficulty range, 2-3 fitting approaches with the fiction for each |
| Puzzle / riddle | the clue chain (fiction), what each approach reveals (Clever reads, Careful searches, Sneaky eavesdrops …), a difficulty |
| Hazard (fire, collapse, curse) | treat it **as a character**: 1-2 aspects, a rating (attack value), stress boxes (0-3), what "taking it out" means |
| Social obstacle (crowd, gatekeeper) | what they want, an aspect, which approaches work and which backfire |
| Challenge (multi-step) | 2-4 overcome steps with different approaches, each with its own difficulty |

## Cost menu (for ties and "success at a cost")
Offer 2-3 costs that fit the fiction: lose time, attract attention, damage gear, take a minor consequence, reveal yourself, owe someone. Costs may feed a hook's `## Surface` but never expose its `## 🔒 Hidden Truth`.

## Output block
```
**Fate**
- Aspect: "<…>"
- Suggested difficulty: <name (+n)> – <why>; raises it: <…> / lowers it: <…>
- Fitting approaches: <Approach> — <how>; <Approach> — <how>
- (If a hazard) Attack: <+n> · Stress: <n boxes> · Taken out when: <…>
- Cost options: <…>; <…>
```
