# FAE: Obstacles

## Choosing a difficulty (suggest, never decide)
| Situation | Suggested passive opposition |
|---|---|
| Easy but not automatic; failure would still be interesting | Average (+1) / Fair (+2) |
| Real challenge for a competent PC | Good (+3) / Great (+4) |
| Heroic, only with aspects invoked | Superb (+5) / Fantastic (+6) |
| Nearly impossible without a plan | Epic (+7)+ |

Rules of thumb: if failure is not interesting, don't call for a roll (say so). Raise difficulty by +2 for each strong, relevant situation aspect working against the PCs. Give a **range**, e.g. "Makul–İyi (+2/+3)", and name what pushes it up or down.

## Passive vs active
- **Passive:** a fixed difficulty (a wall, a lock, a storm).
- **Active:** someone opposes with an approach roll (a guard, a cultist). Suggest the NPC's best approach and rating.

## Obstacle types
| Type | What to produce |
|---|---|
| Barrier (lock, wall, chasm) | 1 aspect, difficulty range, 2-3 fitting approaches with fiction for each |
| Puzzle / riddle | the clue chain (fiction), what each approach reveals (Clever reads, Careful searches, Sneaky eavesdrops…), difficulty |
| Hazard (fire, collapse, curse) | treat **as a character**: 1-2 aspects, a rating (attack value), stress boxes (0-3), what "taking it out" means |
| Social obstacle (crowd, gatekeeper) | what they want, an aspect, which approaches work and which backfire |
| Challenge (multi-step) | 2-4 overcome steps with different approaches, each with its own difficulty |

## Cost menu (for ties and "success at a cost")
Offer 2-3 costs that fit the fiction: lose time, attract attention, damage gear, take a minor consequence, reveal yourself, owe someone. Costs may feed a hook's `## Yüzey` but never expose its `## 🔒 Gizli Gerçek`.
