# d20 Hero: Resolution

## Checks
Roll d20, add the rank or modifier of the fitting trait (ability, skill, power), compare with the **DC**. Meeting or beating it succeeds. You explain; the GM sets the DC and reads the roll.

## Degrees of success and failure
A plain success or failure is one degree; every 5 full points above or below the DC adds one more. Success by 0-4 is one degree, by 5-9 two, and so on; failure by 1-4 is one degree of failure, by 5-9 two, and so on. Many effects (resistance checks, team checks) read the number of degrees. A natural 20 raises the result by one degree (on attacks it always hits and can be a critical hit); a natural 1 on an attack always misses.

## Abilities and skills
| Ability | Covers |
|---|---|
| Strength | lifting, melee damage, Athletics |
| Stamina | health, Fortitude and Toughness |
| Agility | balance, speed, Dodge, initiative, Acrobatics, Stealth |
| Dexterity | hand-eye, ranged attacks, Sleight of Hand, Vehicles |
| Fighting | close attacks, Parry |
| Intellect | reasoning, Expertise, Investigation, Technology, Treatment |
| Awareness | perception, Will, Insight, Perception |
| Presence | personality, Deception, Intimidation, Persuasion |

Skills also include Close Combat and Ranged Combat (bonuses to specific attacks). Rank 0 is an average adult. The trait follows from *how* the character does it, as the player describes it.

## Circumstances
- A minor advantage or drawback: **±2**. A major one: **±5**. Missing tools: −5 (makeshift tools −2).
- **Routine check:** under no pressure, treat the roll as 10.
- **Opposed check:** both roll; the higher total wins (a tie goes to the higher bonus).
- **Team check:** helpers roll the same trait against DC 10; their combined degrees give the leader +2 (one degree of success), +5 (three or more), or −2 (two or more degrees of failure).

## Attacks and resistance
- **Attack:** d20 + attack bonus against the target's defense: **Parry** for close attacks, **Dodge** for ranged ones. Ranged attacks take −2 at medium and −5 at long range.
- **Resistance check:** d20 + a defense (Dodge, Fortitude, Toughness, Will) against the effect's DC, usually **10 + rank** (**15 + rank** for Damage).

## Extra effort
Once per turn, a hero may push beyond their limits for one benefit: an extra standard action; +2 on a check (or improve +2 to +5, or soften a penalty); +1 rank to a power effect, Strength or speed until the next turn; a temporary alternate use of a power (a power stunt); an extra resistance check against an ongoing effect; or a retry where an effect requires it. Cost: at the start of the next turn the hero is fatigued (then exhausted, then incapacitated). Spending a Victory Point then avoids the fatigue.

## Victory Points
Each hero starts each session with 1; unspent points don't carry over. The GM awards more, mostly when a hero's **complication** (a weakness, an obligation, a secret, a relationship) makes trouble. Spending one, as a reaction:
- **Improve roll:** reroll and keep the better; a second roll of 1-10 counts as +10.
- **Edit scene:** add or change a detail of the scene, with the GM's approval.
- **Heroic feat:** gain one rank of an advantage until the end of the next turn.
- **Inspiration:** a hint or clue from the GM.
- **Instant counter:** try to counter an effect used against you.
- **Recover:** remove dazed, fatigued or stunned at once (exhausted becomes fatigued).

## Answer shape for /help-skills
1. **Check:** which trait (ability, skill, power) and the kind of check (skill, attack, resistance, opposed, team).
2. **Fitting traits:** 2-3, best fit first, each with the fiction that makes it fit ("Athletics to climb the girder; Acrobatics to swing across"). The player's description decides; say so.
3. **Suggested DC:** from the table, with the reason; for an effect, 10 + rank.
4. **Degrees:** what one, two and three degrees of success would mean, if they matter.
5. **Options:** circumstance modifiers in the scene, extra effort, a Victory Point use, a matching power or advantage from the hero's file (by name).
