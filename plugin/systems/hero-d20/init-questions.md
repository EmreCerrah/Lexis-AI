# d20 Hero: World Setup Questions

`/world-init` asks these **after** the core questions, as one batch, in the output language. Every one is optional; an unanswered item means "standard".

1. **Power level:** the series' power level (standard: 10; street-level 6-8, cosmic 12+), and the Character Points it gives (15 × PL).
2. **Kinds of powers:** what powers exist in this world and where they come from (mutation, magic, technology, training …)? Kinds only; write-ups stay in the GM's book.
3. **Complications:** any world-wide complications (secret identities matter, powers are illegal, public fear)?
4. **Victory Points:** standard (1 per session, more for complications), more generous, or tighter?
5. **Homebrew:** house rules or changes to the standard rules?

Write the answers to `system.md` using `templates/system.md`. Leave "Standard" where the GM changed nothing. Do not invent house rules; if the GM asks for ideas, mark them `(suggestion)` and get a confirmation in the same init conversation. Never fill the world with content from a published superhero setting: its city, heroes, villains and teams are not part of the rules.
