# System Pack: Superhero d20 Effects Core

A superhero d20 engine from Open Game Content, on top of the world core: power level, ranks, checks with degrees, damage resisted with Toughness, conditions, extra effort and Victory Points. The core produces fiction; this pack adds the numbers. Use this pack when the GM asks for the effects-based superhero d20 rules (third edition, Open Game Content).

## Content scope
Mechanics only: checks, degrees of success, difficulty classes, circumstance modifiers, team checks, resistance checks, damage and conditions, extra effort, Victory Points, power level and character math. No setting content: no city, heroes, villains, teams, organizations or story from any published book. No power, advantage or gadget write-ups; the GM uses their own book for those. Rules are summarized in our own words.

## Glossary
| Role | Term in this system |
|---|---|
| check | check: d20 + rank or modifier against a DC, with degrees of success and failure |
| trait | ability (8), skill, power effect rank |
| difficulty | DC (0 very easy … 40 nigh-impossible); a defense class for attacks; 10 or 15 + rank for resistance |
| scene-tag | circumstance modifier (±2, ±5) or a condition |
| special-ability | power, advantage |
| harm | damage conditions (−1 Toughness penalties, dazed, staggered, incapacitated, dying) |
| meta-currency | Victory Points |

Keep these terms in English in every output language (`AGENTS.md` §3); add a short gloss the first time if the output language differs.

## Seam (core → pack)
1. The skill writes the **fiction block** first, without numbers.
2. Then it appends a **`d20 Hero` block** from this pack.
3. Homebrew in the world's `system.md` overrides anything here.

## Never
- Roll dice, decide outcomes, or spend or award Victory Points on the GM's or players' behalf. You may remind: "this complication could earn a Victory Point".
- Use the source's own names for its point pools or any of its product, setting or character names.
- Write up powers, advantages or gadgets from the source books; name the effect and point the GM to their book.
