# Fate Core: Obstacles

## Choosing a difficulty (suggest, never decide)
| Situation | Suggested passive opposition |
|---|---|
| Easy but not automatic; failure would still be interesting | Average (+1) / Fair (+2) |
| A real challenge for a competent PC | Good (+3) / Great (+4) |
| Heroic, only with aspects invoked | Superb (+5) / Fantastic (+6) |
| Nearly impossible without a plan | Epic (+7) and up |

Rules of thumb: if failure is not interesting, don't call for a roll, and say so. A difficulty about two steps above the PC's skill means they will likely need fate points or help. Raise the difficulty by +2 for each strong, relevant situation aspect working against the PCs. Give a **range**, for example "Fair–Good (+2/+3)", and name what pushes it up or down.

## Passive vs active
- **Passive:** a fixed difficulty (a wall, a lock, a storm).
- **Active:** someone opposes with a skill roll (a guard's Notice against Stealth). Suggest the NPC's skill and its rating.

## Obstacle types
| Type | What to produce |
|---|---|
| Barrier (lock, wall, chasm) | 1 aspect, a difficulty range, 2-3 fitting skills with the fiction for each |
| Puzzle / riddle | the clue chain (fiction), what each skill reveals (Investigate studies, Notice spots, Lore recalls …), a difficulty |
| Hazard (fire, collapse, curse) | treat it **as a character**: 1-2 aspects, a skill it attacks with and its rating, stress boxes (0-3), what "taking it out" means |
| Social obstacle (crowd, gatekeeper) | what they want, an aspect, which skills work (Rapport, Deceive, Provoke, Resources …) and which backfire |
| **Challenge** (one complex task) | 2-4 overcome rolls with different skills, each with its own difficulty; the overall result is read from all of them together |
| **Contest** (a race, a chase, a debate) | the opposing skill; each exchange the higher roll gets a victory (2 with style); a tie adds a twist aspect; first to 3 victories wins |

## Cost menu (for ties and "success at a cost")
Offer 2-3 costs that fit the fiction: lose time, attract attention, damage gear, take a minor consequence, reveal yourself, owe someone. Costs may feed a hook's `## Surface` but never expose its `## 🔒 Hidden Truth`.

## Output block
```
**Fate**
- Aspect: "<…>"
- Suggested difficulty: <name (+n)> – <why>; raises it: <…> / lowers it: <…>
- Fitting skills: <Skill> — <how>; <Skill> — <how>
- (If a hazard) Attack: <Skill +n> · Stress: <n boxes> · Taken out when: <…>
- (If a contest) Opposing skill: <Skill +n> · Victories needed: 3
- Cost options: <…>; <…>
```
