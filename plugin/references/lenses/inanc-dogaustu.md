# Lens: İnanç ve Doğaüstü

## Odak
How faith, cults, magic and supernatural forces respond to the event, and what the event means inside the world's metaphysics as fixed by `## Değişmez Kurallar`.

## Tetikleyiciler
- A sacred place, relic, ritual, priest or cult is involved.
- Magic, a monster, a curse, a dream or an omen appears.
- Something dead, sealed or forbidden is disturbed.
- The event touches a hook whose hidden truth is supernatural.

## Sorular
1. Which `## Değişmez Kurallar` items govern what just happened (how magic works, what the dead can do, what is impossible)?
2. Does any supernatural power notice, wake, weaken or grow stronger?
3. How do the faithful interpret it — sign, sin, miracle, heresy?
4. Does a cult or church gain a reason to act, hide, or recruit?
5. Does the event move a hook closer to its `## 🔒 Gizli Gerçek`? Protect the truth; let signs of it leak only as omens.

## Yayılma kanalları
Priests and confession, cult cells, dreams and visions, omens in nature (animals, weather, decay), sacred texts. Supernatural "knowing" ignores normal distance but must obey the world's rules.

## Tipik yankılar
- An omen appears elsewhere (birds, blood moon, stopped bells).
- A church sends someone; a cult sends someone else, quietly.
- A sealed thing stirs; a ward weakens.
- Believers turn on an outsider or on each other.
- A supernatural being learns a PC's name.

## Tuzaklar
- Every supernatural effect must fit `## Değişmez Kurallar`. If a ripple would need a rule exception, drop it.
- Do not explain the mystery. Omens hint, they do not reveal.
- A hook's hidden truth is fixed canon; supernatural ripples move toward it, never rewrite it.
