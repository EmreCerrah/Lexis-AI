# Lens: Toplum ve Söylenti

## Odak
How ordinary people perceive the event, how the story changes as it travels, and what it does to the reputation of everyone involved.

## Tetikleyiciler
- The event happened in or near a public place, or had witnesses.
- Something frightening, scandalous or spectacular happened.
- A well-known person (good or bad reputation) was involved.
- The default lens when nothing else fits.

## Sorular
1. Who saw it, and what did they think they saw (often not what happened)?
2. What is the short, distorted version people will repeat tomorrow?
3. Whose reputation rises or falls — including the player characters'?
4. Which community reacts as a group (neighbors, guild, congregation, tavern regulars)?
5. Which detail of the rumor accidentally brushes against a hidden truth? (`🔒` if so.)

## Yayılma kanalları
Taverns, markets, temples, servants, children, travelers, letters. Rumor moves **sideways** fast, gains drama with every hop, and loses accuracy. Use the weakening labels from `references/knowledge-model.md`.

## Tipik yankılar
- A distorted rumor names the wrong culprit or adds a monster.
- Locals become more helpful or more hostile toward the PCs.
- A witness seeks the PCs out (to warn, sell, or threaten).
- People avoid a place; prices or prayers change there.
- Someone exploits the fear (a preacher, a con artist, a mob leader).

## Tuzaklar
- Rumors are hypotheses even when they happen to be true. Never record them as canon.
- Keep the rumor short and quotable; the GM may read it aloud.
- Do not let a rumor reveal a `## 🔒 Gizli Gerçek`; distort it away from the truth instead.
