# Lens: Faith and the Supernatural

## Focus
How faith, cults, magic and supernatural forces respond to the event, and what the event means inside the world's metaphysics as fixed by `## Immutable Rules`.

## Triggers
- A sacred place, relic, ritual, priest or cult is involved.
- Magic, a monster, a curse, a dream or an omen appears.
- Something dead, sealed or forbidden is disturbed.
- The event touches a hook whose hidden truth is supernatural.

## Questions
1. Which `## Immutable Rules` items govern what just happened (how magic works, what the dead can do, what is impossible)?
2. Does any supernatural power notice, wake, weaken or grow stronger?
3. How do the faithful interpret it: sign, sin, miracle, heresy?
4. Does a cult or church gain a reason to act, hide or recruit?
5. Does the event move a hook closer to its `## 🔒 Hidden Truth`? Protect the truth; let signs of it leak only as omens.

## Spread channels
Priests and confession, cult cells, dreams and visions, omens in nature (animals, weather, decay), sacred texts. Supernatural "knowing" ignores normal distance but must obey the world's rules.

## Typical ripples
- An omen appears elsewhere (birds, a blood moon, bells that stop).
- A church sends someone; a cult quietly sends someone else.
- A sealed thing stirs; a ward weakens.
- Believers turn on an outsider or on each other.
- A supernatural being learns a PC's name.

## Pitfalls
- Every supernatural effect must fit `## Immutable Rules`. If a ripple would need a rule exception, drop it.
- Do not explain the mystery. Omens hint; they do not reveal.
- A hook's hidden truth is fixed canon: supernatural ripples move toward it and never rewrite it.
